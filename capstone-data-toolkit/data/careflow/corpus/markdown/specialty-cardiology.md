## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This handbook sets out what the Cardiology department accepts and does not accept, how long its services take, what patients are told to do beforehand, which Meridian plans cover which services, and the department's commitment on first-appointment timing.

1.2 The handbook covers three services only: CARD-CONS, CARD-ECHO and CARD-CATH. No other cardiology service may be booked under this handbook.

1.3 This handbook contains no diagnostic or treatment guidance. Clinical decisions rest with the treating clinician.

1.4 Referral turnaround times (acknowledgement, triage and acceptance decisions) are set by the Referral Management Policy and are not restated here. Where this handbook refers to an "accepted referral", acceptance is determined under that policy.

---

## 2. Conditions accepted

2.1 The department accepts adult referrals (patients aged 18 or over on the referral date) for non-emergency assessment where the referring clinician's stated reason falls within one of the following categories:

- (a) chest pain or chest discomfort that is not currently acute;
- (b) palpitations or an irregular pulse reported by the patient or noted by a clinician;
- (c) breathlessness with a suspected cardiac cause;
- (d) a heart murmur identified on examination;
- (e) known or suspected heart failure, for assessment or follow-up;
- (f) raised blood pressure where cardiac involvement is suspected by the referrer;
- (g) follow-up of a previously documented cardiac condition in a patient who is currently stable;
- (h) cardiac assessment requested ahead of a planned non-cardiac procedure.

2.2 A referral must state the reason in one of the categories above. A referral with no stated reason is returned to the referrer under the Referral Management Policy.

2.3 CARD-ECHO and CARD-CATH are booked only on the instruction of the treating cardiologist after a CARD-CONS. They are not bookable directly from a general referral.

---

## 3. Conditions and patients not accepted

3.1 The department does not accept the following:

- (a) any patient presenting with acute or worsening symptoms at the time of referral. These patients are directed to emergency services and must not be booked into a clinic slot;
- (b) patients under 18 years of age, subject to the named exception in 3.2;
- (c) cardiac care connected with pregnancy;
- (d) patients awaiting or recovering from organ transplantation;
- (e) referrals where the only request is a second opinion on a procedure already carried out at another provider;
- (f) referrals with no clinical reason stated (see 2.2).

3.2 **Named exception.** A patient aged 16 or 17 may be accepted if Dr. Ilse Varga approves in writing before booking. Approval is recorded on the referral record. Without it, the referral is declined.

3.3 No other exceptions to section 3 exist. Site managers have no authority to waive it.

## 4. Services and appointment durations

4.1 The Cardiology service line runs between 30 and 90 minutes per appointment.

4.2 The standard new-patient consultation is **30 minutes** (CARD-CONS).

4.3 Services, durations and list prices:

| Code | Service | Type | Duration | List price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CARD-CONS | Cardiology Consultation | Consultation | 30 minutes | $220 |
| CARD-ECHO | Echocardiogram | Imaging | 45 minutes | $950 |
| CARD-CATH | Cardiac Catheterisation | Procedure | 90 minutes | $7,800 |

4.4 Durations are the booked slot lengths held in the scheduling system. Patient arrival and recovery time are additional (see section 5).

## 5. Patient preparation instructions

5.1 Preparation instructions are issued to the patient at the time of booking and again in the reminder sent 2 days before the appointment.

**CARD-CONS (30 minutes)**

5.2 The patient is asked to:

- (a) arrive 10 minutes before the appointment time;
- (b) bring photo identification and their plan membership card;
- (c) bring a written list of all current medications and doses;
- (d) bring copies of any prior cardiac test results held by the patient.

5.3 No fasting is required.

**CARD-ECHO (45 minutes)**

5.4 The patient is asked to:

- (a) arrive 15 minutes before the appointment time;
- (b) wear a two-piece outfit, as the chest must be accessible;
- (c) bring photo identification and their plan membership card.

5.5 No fasting is required.

5.6 Where pre-authorisation applies (section 6), the appointment is confirmed only after the plan's decision is recorded.

**CARD-CATH (90 minutes)**

5.7 The patient is asked to:

- (a) arrive 60 minutes before the appointment time;
- (b) not eat for 6 hours before the appointment; water is permitted until 2 hours before arrival;
- (c) arrange for an adult to accompany them and drive them home. The procedure will not proceed without a named escort;
- (d) bring photo identification, their plan membership card and a written medication list.

5.8 Instructions about continuing or pausing any medication before the procedure are given only by the treating cardiology team, on a case-by-case basis. Booking and front-desk staff must not give them.

## 6. Plan coverage and pre-authorisation

6.1 Meridian offers four plans in this specialty: MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE.

6.2 "Pre-authorisation in N business days" means pre-authorisation is required before the service is delivered, and the plan's decision is processed within N business days of submission.

6.3 Coverage matrix:

| Code | MERIDIAN-GOLD | MERIDIAN-SILVER | MERIDIAN-BRONZE | CIVIC-BASE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CARD-CONS ($220) | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation |
| CARD-ECHO ($950) | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; pre-authorisation in 3 business days | Covered; pre-authorisation in 5 business days | Covered; pre-authorisation in 5 business days |
| CARD-CATH ($7,800) | Covered; pre-authorisation in 2 business days | Covered; pre-authorisation in 3 business days | Covered; pre-authorisation in 5 business days | **Not covered** |

6.4 CARD-CATH is not covered under CIVIC-BASE. Before booking a CARD-CATH for a CIVIC-BASE member, staff must tell the patient that the service is not covered and that the list price is $7,800. The booking proceeds only after the patient acknowledges this and the acknowledgement is recorded.

6.5 Pre-authorisation requests are submitted on the same day the treating cardiologist's instruction is recorded. Staff must not schedule a CARD-ECHO or CARD-CATH as confirmed until the decision is on file, where pre-authorisation applies.

6.6 The longest pre-authorisation wait in this service line is 5 business days. It applies to CARD-ECHO and CARD-CATH under MERIDIAN-BRONZE, and to CARD-ECHO under CIVIC-BASE.

---

## 7. First-appointment commitment

7.1 The department commits to **offering** a first appointment within **7 days** of an accepted referral.

7.2 The 7 days run from the date the referral is recorded as accepted under the Referral Management Policy. They do not run from the date the referrer sent the referral.

7.3 The first appointment is always a CARD-CONS (30 minutes). Because CARD-CONS requires no pre-authorisation under any of the four plans, plan pre-authorisation periods do not delay the 7-day commitment.

7.4 The commitment concerns the date offered, not the date attended. If the patient declines the offered date, the commitment is treated as met, and the declined offer is recorded.

7.5 CARD-ECHO and CARD-CATH are not covered by the 7-day commitment. They are scheduled after the consultation, subject to section 6.

7.6 If no first appointment can be offered within 7 days, the site manager must notify the Operations Policy lead on the same business day.
