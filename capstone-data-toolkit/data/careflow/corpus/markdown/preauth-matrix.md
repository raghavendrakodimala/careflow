# Pre-Authorisation Requirement Matrix

# Meridian Health Partners
## Pre-Authorisation Requirements by Plan and Procedure

**Document ID:** MHP-OPS-PA-014
**Version:** 3.2
**Effective date:** 1 March 2025
**Owner:** Operations Policy Lead
**Applies to:** All 14 Meridian Health Partners sites

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This document states which procedures require prior approval (pre-authorisation) under each of four plans: MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE.

1.2 It covers 24 procedure codes across eight specialties: Cardiology, Orthopaedics, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, Pulmonology, Neurology and ENT. No other codes are in scope. A code not listed in Section 7 must not be submitted for pre-authorisation under this document.

1.3 For every procedure, Section 7 states whether pre-authorisation is required under each plan, the approval window in business days, the clinical documentation needed, and the appeal route if the request is denied.

1.4 The rules in this document match those held in the insurance system. If the system and this document differ, the Operations Policy Lead must be notified within 1 business day. Until the discrepancy is resolved, the system governs.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 **Business day:** Monday to Friday, excluding the 9 public holidays observed by Meridian Health Partners.

2.2 **Approval window:** the maximum number of business days the plan has to issue a decision. It starts on the business day after receipt of a complete submission. A submission received after 16:00 local site time counts as received on the next business day.

2.3 **Complete submission:** a request containing every document listed for the procedure in Section 7. An incomplete submission does not start the approval window.

2.4 **Category:** each code belongs to one of four categories: consultation, lab, imaging or procedure. The category determines the pre-authorisation rule in Section 3.

2.5 **List price:** the Meridian standard charge for the code before any plan adjustment. It is shown in Section 7 for reference and is used for self-pay quotes under clause 8.3.

## 3. Pre-Authorisation Rules by Category

3.1 **Consultations.** No consultation code (CARD-CONS, ORTH-CONS, DERM-CONS, ENDO-CONS, GAST-CONS, PULM-CONS, NEUR-CONS, ENT-CONS) requires pre-authorisation under any plan.

3.2 **Lab work.** No lab code (DERM-BIOP, ENDO-PANL, PULM-PFT, ENT-AUDI) requires pre-authorisation under any plan.

3.3 **Imaging.** Imaging codes (CARD-ECHO, ORTH-MRI, GAST-USND, NEUR-MRI) are treated as follows:
- (a) MERIDIAN-GOLD: pre-authorisation not required.
- (b) MERIDIAN-SILVER: required, approval window 3 business days.
- (c) MERIDIAN-BRONZE: required, approval window 5 business days.
- (d) CIVIC-BASE: required, approval window 5 business days.

3.4 **Procedures.** Procedure codes (CARD-CATH, ORTH-ARTH, DERM-EXCI, ENDO-FNAB, GAST-COLO, PULM-BRON, NEUR-NCS, ENT-SEPT) require pre-authorisation under every plan where they are covered:
- (a) MERIDIAN-GOLD: approval window 2 business days.
- (b) MERIDIAN-SILVER: approval window 3 business days.
- (c) MERIDIAN-BRONZE: approval window 5 business days.
- (d) CIVIC-BASE: approval window 7 business days.

3.5 **Exclusions.** Two codes are not covered at all under CIVIC-BASE: CARD-CATH and ORTH-ARTH. No pre-authorisation request may be submitted for either code under that plan (see Section 8).

3.6 **Validity.** An approval is valid for 60 calendar days from the decision date. If the service is not delivered within that period, a new request is needed.

3.7 **No deemed approval.** If a plan has not decided within the approval window, the request is not approved. The site authorisation coordinator must escalate to the Utilisation Review Duty Manager on the business day after the window expires.

## 4. Submission Standards

4.1 Requests are submitted through the Meridian Authorisation Portal by the ordering clinician or the site authorisation coordinator.

4.2 Each request must identify the plan, the member ID, the procedure code, the ordering clinician and the planned site and date of service.

4.3 Documents must be dated. Where Section 7 states a recency limit (for example, "within 60 days"), the limit is measured back from the submission date.

4.4 Where pre-authorisation is not required, the documentation listed in Section 7 must still be recorded in the patient chart before the service is delivered. It is not submitted to the plan unless the plan asks for it in a post-service audit.

## 5. Documentation Applying to All Pre-Authorisation Requests

5.1 Every request that requires pre-authorisation must include:
- (a) a signed order or referral from the ordering clinician;
- (b) a clinical note dated within 30 days stating the diagnosis or working diagnosis and the clinical reason for the service;
- (c) a current medication list; and
- (d) a record of the relevant allergies.

5.2 Section 7 lists the additional documents specific to each code. Section 5 and Section 7 are cumulative.

## 6. Appeal Routes

6.1 A denial notice states the reason code, the specialty of the reviewing clinician and the appeal deadlines.

6.2 **Route A (MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE).**
- (a) *Level 1, peer-to-peer.* The ordering clinician requests a call with a same-specialty reviewer at the Meridian Utilisation Review Unit within 5 business days of the denial. The reviewer decides within 2 business days of the call.
- (b) *Level 2, written appeal.* If Level 1 does not overturn the denial, a written appeal with any new documentation may be filed with the Meridian Plan Appeals Panel within 30 calendar days of the denial or of the Level 1 outcome, whichever is later. The Panel, chaired by Dr. Tomas Aldereth, decides within 10 business days of receipt.

6.3 **Route B (CIVIC-BASE).**
- (a) *Level 1, peer-to-peer.* The ordering clinician requests a call with a same-specialty Civic Base medical reviewer within 5 business days of the denial. The reviewer decides within 3 business days of the call.
- (b) *Level 2, written appeal.* A written appeal may be filed with the Civic Base Appeals Office within 30 calendar days of the denial or of the Level 1 outcome, whichever is later. The Office decides within 15 business days of receipt.

6.4 **Route C (coverage exclusions).** A denial because a code is not covered is a benefit decision, not a clinical one, so Routes A and B do not apply. The patient or site may file a Coverage Exclusion Query with the Member Benefits Desk within 30 calendar days. The Desk answers within 10 business days. Clinical documentation is not reviewed under Route C.

6.5 **Codes with no pre-authorisation.** Where no pre-authorisation decision exists, there is nothing to appeal under Routes A to C. A disputed claim for such a code follows the claim dispute procedure held by the Billing Resolution Team, led by Marguerite Osei, and must be raised within 60 calendar days of the remittance advice.

## 7. Procedure-by-Procedure Requirements

Wherever "Route A" or "Route B" is cited below, the reviewing clinician is from the named specialty.

### 7A. Cardiology

7.1 **CARD-CONS, Cardiology Consultation** (consultation, list price $220).
- Pre-authorisation: not required under MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE or CIVIC-BASE. Approval window: not applicable.
- Documentation (chart only): referral or self-referral note stating the presenting complaint and its onset date.
- Appeal route: none (clause 6.5).

7.2 **CARD-ECHO, Echocardiogram** (imaging, list price $950).
- Pre-authorisation: not required under MERIDIAN-GOLD. Required under MERIDIAN-SILVER (3 business days), MERIDIAN-BRONZE (5 business days) and CIVIC-BASE (5 business days).
- Documentation: clause 5.1 items; a 12-lead ECG report dated within 90 days; a symptom description with onset date; and, if an echocardiogram was performed in the prior 12 months, the report and a written reason for repeating it.
- Appeal route: Route A (Cardiology reviewer) for the MERIDIAN plans; Route B for CIVIC-BASE.

7.3 **CARD-CATH, Cardiac Catheterisation** (procedure, list price $7,800).
- Pre-authorisation: required under MERIDIAN-GOLD (2 business days), MERIDIAN-SILVER (3 business days) and MERIDIAN-BRONZE (5 business days). Not covered under CIVIC-BASE; no request may be submitted.
- Documentation: clause 5.1 items; a cardiology consultation note dated within 60 days; an ECG report dated within 90 days; a stress test or echocardiogram report dated within 90 days; a serum creatinine result dated within 30 days; and a documented anticoagulant management plan.
- Appeal route: Route A (Cardiology reviewer) for the MERIDIAN plans. For CIVIC-BASE, Route C only.

### 7B. Orthopaedics

7.4 **ORTH-CONS, Orthopaedics Consultation** (consultation, list price $200).
- Pre-authorisation: not required under any of the four plans. Approval window: not applicable.
- Documentation (chart only): referral note identifying the affected joint and side.
- Appeal route: none (clause 6.5).

7.5 **ORTH-MRI, MRI Knee** (imaging, list price $1,400).
- Pre-authorisation: not required under MERIDIAN-GOLD. Required under MERIDIAN-SILVER (3 business days), MERIDIAN-BRONZE (5 business days) and CIVIC-BASE (5 business days).
- Documentation: clause 5.1 items; a knee examination note dated within 30 days; a weight-bearing radiograph report dated within 60 days; a record of at least 6 weeks of conservative management (or a documented reason it was not attempted, such as a locked knee); and a completed MRI safety screening form.
- Appeal route: Route A (Orthopaedics reviewer) for the MERIDIAN plans; Route B for CIVIC-BASE.

7.6 **ORTH-ARTH, Knee Arthroscopy** (procedure, list price $6,200).
- Pre-authorisation: required under MERIDIAN-GOLD (2 business days), MERIDIAN-SILVER (3 business days) and MERIDIAN-BRONZE (5 business days). Not covered under CIVIC-BASE; no request may be submitted.
- Documentation: clause 5.1 items; a knee MRI report dated within 90 days; a record of at least 12 weeks of conservative management; a surgeon's operative-indication note; and a completed anaesthesia pre-assessment form.
- Appeal route: Route A (Orthopaedics reviewer) for the MERIDIAN plans. For CIVIC-BASE, Route C only.

### 7C. Dermatology

7.7 **DERM-CONS, Dermatology Consultation** (consultation, list price $160).
- Pre-authorisation: not required under any of the four plans. Approval window: not applicable.
- Documentation (chart only): referral note with lesion or rash location and duration.
- Appeal route: none (clause 6.5).

7.8 **DERM-BIOP, Skin Biopsy Pathology** (lab, list price $380).
- Pre-authorisation: not required under any of the four plans. Approval window: not applicable.
- Documentation (chart only): a pathology order stating biopsy site, lesion size in millimetres and dermoscopy findings.
- Appeal route: none (clause 6.5).
