# Pulmonology Service Line Handbook

# MERIDIAN HEALTH PARTNERS

**Document ID:** SLH-PULM-01 | **Version:** 3.2 | **Effective:** 1 March 2025 | **Owner:** Operations Policy | **Clinical lead:** Dr. Anselm Voorhees | **Department coordinator:** Teodora Marchetti | **Next review:** 1 March 2026

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This handbook sets out what the Pulmonology service line accepts, how long its services take, what patients are told before attending, which Meridian plans cover which services, and the department's own scheduling commitment.

1.2 It applies to all Meridian sites that offer Pulmonology services, as shown in the scheduling system.

1.3 This handbook contains no diagnostic or treatment guidance. Clinical decisions rest with the treating clinician.

1.4 Clinic-wide referral turnaround times are set by the Referral Management Policy (RMP-02). This handbook does not restate them. If this handbook and RMP-02 appear to differ on referral turnaround, RMP-02 prevails.

## 2. Services in this service line

2.1 The Pulmonology service line comprises three services, and only these three. No other procedure may be booked under Pulmonology without an amendment to this handbook.

| Code | Service | Type | Duration | List price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PULM-CONS | Pulmonology Consultation | Consultation | 30 minutes | $200 |
| PULM-PFT | Pulmonary Function Test | Lab | 45 minutes | $310 |
| PULM-BRON | Bronchoscopy | Procedure | 60 minutes | $3,400 |

2.2 Service durations across the line range from 30 to 60 minutes.

2.3 The standard new-patient consultation is 30 minutes (PULM-CONS). The scheduling system does not extend this slot automatically. A longer visit requires a separately booked service from the table in 2.1.

## 3. Referrals accepted

3.1 The department accepts referrals for adult patients (aged 16 and over) in the following categories:

- (a) persistent cough lasting more than 8 weeks;
- (b) shortness of breath referred for specialist evaluation;
- (c) abnormal findings on chest imaging referred for specialist review;
- (d) established asthma referred for specialist review;
- (e) established chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) referred for specialist review;
- (f) established interstitial lung disease referred for specialist review;
- (g) pre-procedure assessment where a referring clinician has requested bronchoscopy.

3.2 A referral must include the referring clinician's name, the patient's plan code, and the reason for referral. Incomplete referrals are returned to the referrer under the process in RMP-02.

3.3 Acceptance of a referral is recorded in the scheduling system as the "accepted" status. The commitment in section 7 runs from that record.

## 4. Referrals not accepted

4.1 The department does not accept the following. Referrals in these categories are declined, and the referrer is notified by the department coordinator.

- (a) Patients under 16 years of age.
- (b) Patients who are acutely unwell and require emergency or inpatient care. Referrers must use emergency services.
- (c) Lung transplant evaluation.
- (d) Thoracic surgery consultation.
- (e) Sleep-disordered breathing referrals, which are directed to the Sleep Medicine service line.
- (f) Supervision of long-term home ventilation.
- (g) Occupational lung disease assessments requested for legal or compensation claims.
- (h) Referrals with no stated reason for referral.

4.2 Exceptions to section 4 may be granted only in writing by the clinical lead, Dr. Voorhees. The coordinator holds no exception authority.

## 5. Patient preparation instructions

5.1 Preparation instructions are issued in the appointment confirmation letter and repeated in the reminder message sent 3 days before the appointment. Staff must not give instructions that differ from those below.

5.2 **All appointments (PULM-CONS, PULM-PFT, PULM-BRON):**

- (a) Bring photo identification and the Meridian or CIVIC-BASE plan card.
- (b) Bring a written list of current medications, including inhalers.
- (c) Bring any chest imaging discs or reports held by the patient.
- (d) Do not stop or change any medication because of this handbook. Any medication instruction comes from the ordering clinician and is printed in the confirmation letter.
- (e) Reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours before the appointment. Notice given later than 24 hours is recorded as a late cancellation.

5.3 **PULM-CONS (30 minutes):** Arrive 15 minutes before the appointment time to complete registration.

5.4 **PULM-PFT (45 minutes):**

- (a) Arrive 20 minutes before the appointment time.
- (b) Wear loose, comfortable clothing.
- (c) Do not smoke for at least 4 hours before the test.
- (d) Do not eat a large meal within 2 hours before the test.
- (e) If the confirmation letter instructs the patient to withhold an inhaler before the test, the letter states the hours. Otherwise the patient takes inhalers as usual.

5.5 **PULM-BRON (60 minutes):**

- (a) Arrive 45 minutes before the procedure time.
- (b) Do not eat or drink for 6 hours before the procedure unless the confirmation letter states otherwise.
- (c) Arrange for an adult escort to accompany the patient home. The procedure is not started without a named escort recorded on the day.
- (d) The patient must not drive or operate machinery for 24 hours after the procedure.
- (e) The procedure is booked only after pre-authorisation has been approved, where required (see section 6).

## 6. Plan coverage and pre-authorisation

6.1 Meridian operates four plans: MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE. All three Pulmonology services are covered under all four plans.

6.2 Coverage and pre-authorisation by service:

| Code | Service | MERIDIAN-GOLD | MERIDIAN-SILVER | MERIDIAN-BRONZE | CIVIC-BASE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PULM-CONS | Pulmonology Consultation ($200) | Covered, no pre-authorisation | Covered, no pre-authorisation | Covered, no pre-authorisation | Covered, no pre-authorisation |
| PULM-PFT | Pulmonary Function Test ($310) | Covered, no pre-authorisation | Covered, no pre-authorisation | Covered, no pre-authorisation | Covered, no pre-authorisation |
| PULM-BRON | Bronchoscopy ($3,400) | Covered, pre-authorisation in 2 business days | Covered, pre-authorisation in 3 business days | Covered, pre-authorisation in 5 business days | Covered, pre-authorisation in 7 business days |

6.3 The pre-authorisation times in 6.2 are the time the plan takes to decide once a complete request is lodged. They are expressed in business days.

6.4 Staff must lodge the pre-authorisation request for PULM-BRON on the day the clinician orders the procedure. A bronchoscopy slot is confirmed to the patient only after approval is recorded.

6.5 No pre-authorisation is requested for PULM-CONS or PULM-PFT under any of the four plans.

## 7. First-appointment commitment

7.1 The department commits to offering a first appointment within 8 days of an accepted referral.

7.2 The 8 days are counted in calendar days, starting on the date the referral is recorded as accepted (see 3.3).

7.3 The commitment is met when an appointment date within the 8 days is offered to the patient, whether or not the patient takes it. A patient who chooses a later date remains within the commitment.

7.4 The commitment applies to the first appointment, which is PULM-CONS. It does not apply to PULM-PFT or PULM-BRON, or to the time taken by plan pre-authorisation under section 6.

7.5 Where no slot within the 8 days exists, the coordinator, Teodora Marchetti, records the breach in the scheduling system and informs the clinical lead.

7.6 The time between receipt of a referral and its acceptance is governed by RMP-02 and is not part of the 8 days.
