### 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This handbook sets out what the Dermatology department accepts and does not accept, how long its services take, what patients are told before attending, which Meridian plans cover its services, and the department's commitment on first-appointment timing.

1.2 This handbook contains no diagnostic or treatment guidance. Clinical decisions rest with the treating clinician.

1.3 The scheduling and insurance systems hold the same service codes, durations, list prices and plan rules as this handbook. If a system and this handbook differ, report the discrepancy to Operations Policy within 1 business day. Do not resolve it locally.

---

### 2. Services Provided

2.1 The department delivers three services. No other procedures are offered under this service line.

| Code | Service | Type | Duration | List price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DERM-CONS | Dermatology Consultation | Consultation | 20 minutes | $160 |
| DERM-BIOP | Skin Biopsy Pathology | Lab | 20 minutes | $380 |
| DERM-EXCI | Lesion Excision | Procedure | 45 minutes | $1,150 |

2.2 Department services run from 20 minutes (DERM-CONS, DERM-BIOP) to 45 minutes (DERM-EXCI).

2.3 The standard new-patient consultation is 20 minutes (DERM-CONS). Schedulers must not book a new-patient consultation at any other length without written approval from the Service Line Operations Manager.

---

### 3. Conditions Accepted

3.1 The department accepts referrals for the following categories of presenting concern, subject to clause 4:

- (a) Acne and acne-related scarring concerns referred for assessment
- (b) Eczema and dermatitis
- (c) Psoriasis
- (d) Rosacea
- (e) Warts and other common viral skin lesions
- (f) Skin lesions that are new, changing or of uncertain nature, referred for assessment
- (g) Benign skin lesions referred for excision
- (h) Hair loss and scalp conditions
- (i) Nail disorders
- (j) Non-urgent bacterial or fungal skin conditions
- (k) Rashes of unknown cause that are not urgent

3.2 A referral is "accepted" when the department triage clinician records acceptance in the referral system. The 14-day commitment in clause 7 runs from that recorded date.

3.3 Minimum patient age for acceptance is 12 years at the date of the first appointment. Patients aged 12 to 17 must attend with a parent or legal guardian.

---

### 4. Conditions and Requests Not Accepted

4.1 The department does not accept the following. Referrers are told of the decision in the referral outcome notice.

- (a) Acute severe skin reactions or blistering illness requiring same-day hospital care. These patients are directed to emergency services.
- (b) Burns of any depth.
- (c) Chronic leg ulcers and other chronic wounds, which are directed to the wound-care pathway.
- (d) Patients under 12 years of age.
- (e) Cosmetic-only requests, including cosmetic lesion removal and appearance-driven treatments with no clinical referral reason.
- (f) Surgery for lesions that the department's triage clinician has noted as requiring specialist surgical facilities beyond the clinic setting.
- (g) Second-opinion requests where no clinical records have been supplied with the referral.
- (h) Patients seeking a repeat of a procedure completed elsewhere within the previous 90 days, without a written referral reason.

4.2 Named exception: a patient aged 10 or 11 may be accepted only on the written approval of Dr. Verhoeven, recorded in the referral system before booking. No other exceptions to clause 4.1(d) exist.

4.3 Clinic-wide referral handling, including triage turnaround and notification timing to referrers, is governed by the **Referral Management Policy**. This handbook does not restate those timings. In any conflict, the Referral Management Policy applies.

### 5. Patient Preparation Instructions

5.1 The following instructions are given to every patient at booking and again in the appointment reminder message.

**All appointments (DERM-CONS, DERM-BIOP, DERM-EXCI)**

- (a) Arrive 10 minutes before the appointment time to complete check-in.
- (b) Bring photo identification and your plan membership card.
- (c) Bring a written list of current medications and known allergies.
- (d) Bring any referral letter, prior reports or photographs provided by your referrer.
- (e) Do not apply make-up, tinted moisturiser or nail polish to the area being seen.
- (f) Wear clothing that can be removed or moved easily to expose the area being seen.

**DERM-BIOP and DERM-EXCI only**

- (g) Confirm with the front desk that your plan authorisation, if required, has been recorded. The front desk contacts you if it has not.
- (h) Bring a companion if you wish. Companions may wait in the reception area.

**DERM-EXCI only**

- (i) The scheduled slot is 45 minutes. Allow additional time for check-in and for any post-appointment paperwork.
- (j) Procedure appointments are not confirmed until pre-authorisation is recorded (clause 6.4).

5.2 Clinical advice about medication, skin care or activity before a procedure comes only from the treating clinician and is not part of this handbook.

5.3 Cancellation or change: patients are asked to give at least 24 hours' notice. For DERM-EXCI, notice of at least 2 business days is requested so that the slot can be reallocated.

### 6. Plan Coverage

6.1 Meridian operates four plans: MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE.

6.2 Coverage and pre-authorisation for Dermatology services are as follows.

| Code | Service | MERIDIAN-GOLD | MERIDIAN-SILVER | MERIDIAN-BRONZE | CIVIC-BASE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DERM-CONS | Dermatology Consultation | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation |
| DERM-BIOP | Skin Biopsy Pathology | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation |

- MERIDIAN-GOLD: 2 business days

6.6 Coverage means the service is within the plan's benefits. Patient cost-sharing amounts are held in each plan's benefit schedule and are not stated in this handbook.

### 7. First-Appointment Commitment

7.1 The Dermatology department commits to offering a first appointment within **14 calendar days** of an accepted referral (clause 3.2).

7.2 "Offered" means that the patient has been given at least one available DERM-CONS slot within that period. It does not require the patient to accept or attend the slot.

7.3 If the department cannot offer a slot within 14 calendar days, the Service Line Operations Manager is notified on the day the breach is identified and records the reason in the scheduling system.

7.4 The 14-day commitment applies to the first DERM-CONS appointment only. Follow-up scheduling for DERM-BIOP and DERM-EXCI depends on clinical recommendation and, for DERM-EXCI, on the pre-authorisation timings in clause 6.3.

7.5 Triage and referral-handling turnaround times that precede acceptance are set by the Referral Management Policy and are outside the 14-day commitment.
