### 1. Purpose

1.1 This standard defines what non-clinical staff and automated systems may and may not communicate to a patient.

1.2 The standard exists because answers about health, medication or test results given by anyone other than a licensed clinician create patient safety risk and liability for Meridian.

### 2. Scope and Definitions

2.1 **Non-clinical staff** means any employee, contractor, temporary worker or volunteer who is not a Licensed Clinician. This includes front desk coordinators, schedulers, billing representatives, contact centre agents, medical records staff, interpreters, and site managers who are not licensed.

2.2 **Licensed Clinician** means a physician, nurse practitioner, physician assistant, registered nurse, licensed practical nurse, pharmacist, or other professional holding a current licence that Meridian Credentialing has verified. Medical assistants are **not** Licensed Clinicians for the purposes of this standard.

2.3 **Automated system** means any chatbot, interactive voice response (IVR), text-message responder, portal assistant, email auto-responder, or scripted workflow tool that communicates with patients without a human composing each message. This includes systems that use generative language models.

2.4 **Clinical Question** means any patient communication that falls within one or more categories listed in Section 5.

2.5 **Deflection** means the mandatory sequence in Section 6: delivering the Deflection Script, creating a Clinical Routing Ticket, and ending the clinical portion of the exchange.

2.6 **Nurse Line** means the licensed-nurse queue at each site, operating 07:00 to 19:00 local time, Monday to Friday, and 08:00 to 13:00 Saturday. The Central Nurse Queue covers all other hours.

### 3. Information Non-Clinical Staff and Automated Systems MAY Provide

3.1 Staff and systems may provide the following, and only the following, categories of information:

| Ref | Category | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1.1 | Appointment date, time, location, and provider name | Only after identity verification under OP-CS-003 |
| 3.1.2 | Site address, hours, parking, accessibility features, and public transport directions | None |
| 3.1.3 | Insurance acceptance, copay amounts on file, billing balances, and payment options | Only after identity verification |
| 3.1.4 | Whether a test result is **available** in the patient portal | Staff may state only "available" or "not yet available". Staff may **not** describe, characterise or hint at the content or significance of a result. |
| 3.1.5 | Whether a refill request or referral has been **received**, **sent to the pharmacy**, or **is pending clinician review** | Status only. No comment on appropriateness, timing of effect, or dose. |
| 3.1.6 | Pharmacy name and address on file | None |
| 3.1.7 | Pre-visit preparation instructions (fasting, arrival time, items to bring) | Staff must read the text of the current Appointment Preparation Sheet **verbatim**. Staff may not paraphrase, extend, or answer follow-up questions about it. Follow-up questions are Clinical Questions. |
| 3.1.8 | Instructions for using the portal, requesting records, and completing forms | None |
| 3.1.9 | The existence and name of services offered at a site | No statement about whether a service is suitable for the patient's condition |

3.2 Information not listed in 3.1 may not be provided in response to a patient question unless it is released by a Licensed Clinician through a Clinical Routing Ticket.

### 4. Information Non-Clinical Staff and Automated Systems MAY NOT Provide

4.1 Staff and systems may not, under any circumstance:

- (a) interpret, explain, rank, reassure about, or express concern about a symptom;
- (b) confirm or deny that something is "normal", "common", "serious", "nothing to worry about", or similar;
- (c) name, suggest, or rule out a diagnosis or condition;
- (d) state or confirm a medication name, purpose, dose, frequency, timing, side effect, interaction, or whether a dose may be skipped, doubled or adjusted;
- (e) read back a result value, flag, range, or the words "normal" or "abnormal";
- (f) advise whether to attend an appointment, go to an emergency department, wait, or seek care elsewhere, except by delivering the Emergency Statement in clause 7.2;
- (g) relay a clinician's earlier advice from memory or from a chart note, unless the Clinical Routing Ticket response authorises the exact wording;
- (h) suggest that a clinician "would probably say" anything;
- (i) answer using general knowledge, internet sources, or generative output.

4.2 Offering sympathy ("I'm sorry you're not feeling well") is permitted. Offering any statement of probability or outcome is not.

### 5. Categories of Question That Must Be Routed to a Licensed Clinician

5.1 Every communication containing any of the following **must** be deflected and routed. No staff judgment about "how simple" or "how obvious" the question is applies.

| Cat. | Name | Includes (non-exhaustive examples) |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Symptom interpretation | "Is this rash normal?", "Why does my chest feel tight?", "How long does a fever like this last?", "Is this bruise too big?" |
| C2 | Medication questions | Purpose, side effects, interactions, missed doses, storage effects, substitution, alcohol or food interactions, whether a brand and generic are equivalent |
| C3 | Dosage | Any question about amount, number of tablets, frequency, timing, tapering, or splitting |
| C4 | Test-result meaning | Any request to explain, summarise, compare, or give an opinion on a result, including "Is it bad?" and "Do I need to worry?" |
| C5 | "Should I" questions | Any question phrased as, or functionally equivalent to, "should I", "do I need to", "is it safe to", "can I still", "would you recommend", "what would you do", "is it okay if I" when the subject is health, treatment, activity, or attendance |
| C6 | Post-procedure and post-visit care | Wound care, activity restrictions, return-to-work timing, bathing, driving, diet changes |
| C7 | Pregnancy, paediatric and infant health questions | Any health question about a pregnancy, child, or infant |
| C8 | Mental-health content | Any statement or question about mood, self-harm, or distress, in addition to the Emergency Statement where triggered |

5.2 **Category C5 rule.** If a question contains "should I" (or an equivalent in clause 5.1, C5) and a health subject, it is a Clinical Question even if the staff member believes the answer is administrative. If a "should I" question is purely administrative ("Should I bring my insurance card?"), the staff member may answer only from the Appointment Preparation Sheet or clause 3.1. If the answer is not in those sources, treat it as C5.

5.3 **Mixed communications.** If a call or message contains both an administrative request and a Clinical Question, staff must complete the administrative request, then deflect the Clinical Question. Staff must not let the administrative answer carry any implied clinical meaning.

5.4 **Unclear cases.** If a staff member is unsure whether a question is clinical, it is clinical. Deflect.

### 6. Mandatory Deflection Procedure and Script

6.1 On identifying a Clinical Question, staff must carry out the following steps in order:

1. Deliver the Deflection Script (clause 6.2) without alteration.
2. Create a Clinical Routing Ticket in the Meridian Contact System within **3 minutes** of the end of the contact.
3. Confirm the patient's best callback number and a safe time window.
4. State the callback commitment in clause 6.4.
5. End the clinical portion of the contact.

6.2 **Deflection Script (voice, in person, live chat).** Staff must say the following exactly:

> "I'm not able to answer that, because it needs a licensed clinician. I'm sending your question to a nurse at your clinic now, and a nurse will call you back within four business hours. Can I confirm the best number to reach you?"

6.3 **Deflection Script (text message and automated systems).**

> "We can't answer questions about symptoms, medications, doses, test results, or what you should do. A licensed nurse will contact you within 4 business hours. If you think this is an emergency, call 911 now."

6.4 **Callback commitment.** A Licensed Clinician must contact the patient within **4 business hours** of ticket creation. Tickets created after 17:00 or on a non-business day carry the deadline of 11:00 on the next business day. The Nurse Supervisor must be alerted automatically at 3 hours.

6.5 **Prohibited additions.** Staff must not append a reason, a hint, or a personal view to the script. The following are examples of prohibited additions: "but I'm sure it's fine", "they'll probably tell you the same as last time", "that sounds worrying".

6.6 **Repeating the script.** If the patient presses for an answer, staff must repeat the Deflection Script (first sentence only, then the callback commitment) up to **three times**. After the third repetition, staff must offer a warm transfer to the Nurse Line or Central Nurse Queue. Staff must not escalate by giving a partial answer.

6.7 **Permitted wording variation.** Interpreter-delivered versions must use the approved translation held in the Language Services library (version-matched to this document). Any other variation is a breach.

### 7. Emergency Statement (Parallel Obligation, Not an Exception)

7.1 If a patient states, in any words, that they are experiencing any of the following, staff must deliver the Emergency Statement in clause 7.2 **in addition to** carrying out Section 6: chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke, loss of consciousness, severe bleeding, overdose or poisoning, thoughts of harming themselves or others, or a child or infant who is unresponsive.

7.2 **Emergency Statement:**

> "If this is an emergency, please hang up and call 911 now, or go to the nearest emergency department."

7.3 Staff must not ask diagnostic questions, assess severity, or comment on whether the situation is an emergency. The Emergency Statement is delivered on the patient's own words alone.

7.4 For a voice contact, staff must then page the on-duty nurse through the Urgent Page function. The nurse must respond within **15 minutes**.

7.5 The Emergency Statement is a scripted statement. It is not clinical advice and does not constitute an exception to Section 5 or Section 8.

### 8. No Exceptions

8.1 **This rule has no exceptions.** No staff member, manager, site director, or system administrator may authorise a departure from Sections 4, 5 or 6.

8.2 **Repeat callers.** The rule applies in full to a patient who has contacted Meridian two, five, or fifty times about the same issue. Staff must not answer on the basis that "they already know" or "they were told last time". On the **third** contact on the same issue within **72 hours**, staff must additionally flag the Clinical Routing Ticket as "Repeat – Priority" and notify the Nurse Supervisor by direct message within 10 minutes. This flag changes the priority of the clinician callback. It does not change what staff may say.

8.3 **Stated healthcare professionals.** The rule applies in full when a caller states that they are a physician, nurse, pharmacist, paramedic, medical student, or any other healthcare professional, or that they work for another provider or for Meridian. Staff must not verify, challenge, or act on the claim. Staff must deliver the Deflection Script and create a Clinical Routing Ticket marked "Caller states HCP". The licensed clinician who receives the ticket decides how the contact is handled, including whether the caller is verified and what is shared.

8.4 **Other callers.** The rule also applies without exception to the following: family members and caregivers (subject also to OP-CS-003 on authorisation), employers, insurers, legal representatives, and Meridian executives.

8.5 **Time pressure.** High call volume, queue length, system downtime, or end-of-day does not suspend the rule. During system downtime, staff must record the Clinical Question on the paper Downtime Routing Form (Form CS-14B) and enter it into the system within **60 minutes** of restoration.

8.6 **Patient distress or insistence.** Patient frustration, tears, anger, or a statement such as "just tell me yes or no" does not alter the requirement.

### 9. Automated Systems: Additional Requirements

9.1 No automated system may generate, retrieve, or display content that answers a Clinical Question. Generative components must be configured with a clinical-content block.

9.2 **Classifier threshold.** Every inbound patient message must be scored by the Clinical Content Classifier. Any message scoring **0.30 or above** for any category C1–C8 must trigger the Deflection Script in clause 6.3. The threshold may be changed only by the CMO in writing.

9.3 **Fail-closed behaviour.** If the classifier is unavailable, times out after **2 seconds**, or returns an error, the system must deflect. It must not respond to free-text patient messages with any content other than the clause 6.3 script and a link to the appointment menu.

9.4 **Hard-coded content.** An automated system may deliver only content in clause 3.1 that was approved by the Content Review Group and carries a content ID. The system must log the content ID on each response.

9.5 **Portal result notifications.** An automated notification that a result is available must use only the text: "A new result is available in your portal." Notifications must not include result values, flags, colours, icons or adjectives.

9.6 **Handoff.** Where a patient message triggers deflection, the system must create a Clinical Routing Ticket automatically and display the 4-business-hour callback commitment.

9.7 **Change control.** No change to an automated system's patient-facing logic goes live until the Operations Policy Lead and a Licensed Clinician from the Clinical Content Panel have signed the change record. Emergency rollbacks may be performed by the on-call engineer and must be reported to the Operations Policy Lead within 24 hours.

### 10. Logging, Audit and Monitoring

10.1 Every deflection must be logged with: date and time, site, staff ID or system ID, category code (C1–C8), whether the Emergency Statement was delivered, and ticket number.

10.2 **Monthly audit.** The Quality and Compliance team must review, for each site, **50** randomly selected contacts that were logged as deflected and **50** that were not. The purpose is to detect missed deflections. The target is a **missed-deflection rate of 0%**; any rate above **1%** at a site triggers a site-level corrective action plan within 10 business days.

10.3 **Call recording review.** At least **5%** of Central Contact Centre calls must be reviewed against this standard each month.

10.4 **Automated system testing.** Each automated system must be tested quarterly with the Clinical Question Test Set, containing a minimum of **400** prompts across C1–C8. The pass requirement is **100%** deflection of prompts labelled clinical.

### 11. Training and Certification

11.1 No person may handle patient contacts until they have completed the OP-CS-014 module (duration 75 minutes) and passed the assessment at **90% or higher**.

11.2 Recertification is required every **12 months**. A lapse of more than **14 days** removes the person's patient-contact access until recertified.

11.3 Training must include at least **10** role-play scenarios, including a repeat caller and a caller who claims to be a healthcare professional.

### 12. Breaches

12.1 Breaches are classified as follows:

| Level | Definition | Required response |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Script wording varied; no clinical content given | Coaching within 5 business days; logged |
| Level 2 | Clinical content given, no evident patient harm | Written warning; retraining within 10 business days; Nurse Supervisor to contact the patient within 1 business day |
| Level 3 | Clinical content given and patient harm reported or reasonably suspected | Immediate removal from patient-contact duties; incident report within 24 hours to the CMO; disciplinary process under HR-007 |
| Level S | Automated system gave clinical content | System taken out of service within 4 hours or placed in deflect-all mode; root-cause report to the CMO within 5 business days |

12.2 A staff member who believes they have breached this standard must report it to their Nurse Supervisor within **1 hour** of the contact ending. Prompt self-reporting is a mitigating factor in assessing breach level.

12.3 Managers must not instruct, encourage, or tolerate a departure from this standard. Doing so is itself a Level 3 breach by the manager.
