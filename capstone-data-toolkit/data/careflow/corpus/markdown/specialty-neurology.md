# Neurology Service Line Handbook

# Meridian Health Partners
## Service Line Handbook: Neurology
**Document ref:** MHP-SLH-NEUR-01
**Owner:** Operations Policy
**Clinical lead:** Dr. Ilse Varga, Head of Neurology
**Applies to:** all Meridian Health Partners sites offering Neurology services

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This handbook sets out what the Neurology service line accepts and does not accept, how long appointments run, what patients are told to do before attending, how the four Meridian plans treat each Neurology procedure, and the department's commitment on first-appointment timing.

1.2 This handbook contains no diagnostic or treatment guidance. Clinical decisions rest with the treating neurologist.

1.3 Referral intake, triage and turnaround times for the clinic as a whole are set by the Referral Management Policy. Where this handbook mentions referral timing, the Referral Management Policy governs. This handbook does not restate those times.

---

## 2. Conditions accepted

2.1 The Neurology service line accepts adult referrals (patients aged 16 and over at the date of referral) for specialist assessment of the following condition groups:

| Ref | Condition group |
|---|---|
| 2.1.1 | Recurrent or persistent headache disorders |
| 2.1.2 | Seizure disorders and suspected seizure disorders, non-emergency |
| 2.1.3 | Peripheral neuropathy and suspected nerve entrapment |
| 2.1.4 | Movement disorders, including tremor |
| 2.1.5 | Multiple sclerosis, outpatient assessment and ongoing review |
| 2.1.6 | Memory or cognitive concerns referred for neurological assessment |
| 2.1.7 | Dizziness or balance symptoms where a neurological cause is queried |
| 2.1.8 | Numbness, tingling or weakness of unclear cause |

2.2 A referral must name one of the groups above or give a clinical question that falls within one of them. The Referral Management Policy governs how referrals are accepted or returned.

---

## 3. Conditions not accepted

3.1 The following are not accepted by the Neurology service line at any Meridian site:

| Ref | Not accepted | Redirect |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1.1 | Suspected acute stroke, or any presentation needing emergency care | Emergency services |
| 3.1.2 | Active or prolonged seizure | Emergency services |
| 3.1.3 | Patients under 16 years of age | Referring clinician to arrange paediatric services outside Meridian |
| 3.1.4 | Conditions where the primary question is surgical (neurosurgical opinion) | Referring clinician to arrange externally |
| 3.1.5 | Primary psychiatric presentations with no neurological question | Meridian Behavioural Health intake |
| 3.1.6 | Requests for imaging or testing alone, without a consultation | Not offered as a standalone pathway |
| 3.1.7 | Medico-legal or insurance-assessment reports | Not offered |

3.2 Exception to 3.1.6: NEUR-MRI and NEUR-NCS may be booked only where the requesting clinician is a Meridian neurologist who has seen the patient, or where the referral names the test and has been accepted under the Referral Management Policy.

3.3 Exception to 3.1.3: none. No site-level or clinician-level override exists for patients under 16.

---

## 4. Appointment duration

4.1 The standard new-patient consultation (NEUR-CONS) is **45 minutes**.

4.2 Services in this specialty run between **45 and 60 minutes**, as follows:

| Code | Service | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| NEUR-CONS | Neurology Consultation | 45 minutes |
| NEUR-MRI | MRI Brain | 45 minutes |
| NEUR-NCS | Nerve Conduction Study | 60 minutes |

4.3 Scheduling holds the full listed duration for each booking. Durations are not shortened at booking.

---

## 5. Preparation instructions given to patients

5.1 Instructions are issued in the appointment confirmation and repeated in the reminder message. They are administrative and practical in nature and do not replace instructions from the treating clinician.

**All Neurology appointments**

5.2 Patients are asked to:
- (a) bring photo identification and their plan membership card;
- (b) bring a current list of medications, including doses;
- (c) bring any prior scan reports or test results they hold;
- (d) arrive 15 minutes before the appointment time for registration;
- (e) bring a companion if they wish, who may attend the consultation with the patient's consent.

**NEUR-CONS Neurology Consultation**

5.3 Patients are asked to note the date and description of their symptoms beforehand so they can describe them during the 45-minute appointment.

**NEUR-MRI MRI Brain**

5.4 Patients are asked to:
- (a) complete the MRI safety questionnaire sent with the confirmation, and return it before arrival;
- (b) tell the department in advance if they have any implanted device, pacemaker or metal fragments;
- (c) leave jewellery, watches and removable metal items at home or in the locker provided;
- (d) wear clothing without metal fasteners.

**NEUR-NCS Nerve Conduction Study**

5.5 Patients are asked to:
- (a) wear loose clothing that allows access to the arms or legs;
- (b) arrive with clean, dry skin, with no lotions, oils or creams on the limbs to be tested;
- (c) tell the department in advance if they have an implanted device, as this must be reviewed before the appointment.

5.6 Where pre-authorisation is required (section 6), the appointment is not confirmed to the patient as final until pre-authorisation has been recorded.

## 6. Procedures and plan coverage

6.1 Meridian offers four plans: MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE. All three Neurology procedures are covered under all four plans. The plans differ only in pre-authorisation.

6.2 List prices:

| Code | Procedure | Category | List price |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEUR-CONS | Neurology Consultation | Consultation | $230 |
| NEUR-MRI | MRI Brain | Imaging | $1,650 |
| NEUR-NCS | Nerve Conduction Study | Procedure | $780 |

6.3 Coverage and pre-authorisation by plan. "Pre-authorisation in N business days" means the plan's pre-authorisation decision time for that procedure.

| Code | MERIDIAN-GOLD | MERIDIAN-SILVER | MERIDIAN-BRONZE | CIVIC-BASE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NEUR-CONS | Covered, no pre-authorisation | Covered, no pre-authorisation | Covered, no pre-authorisation | Covered, no pre-authorisation |
| NEUR-MRI | Covered, no pre-authorisation | Covered, pre-authorisation in 3 business days | Covered, pre-authorisation in 5 business days | Covered, pre-authorisation in 5 business days |
| NEUR-NCS | Covered, pre-authorisation in 2 business days | Covered, pre-authorisation in 3 business days | Covered, pre-authorisation in 5 business days | Covered, pre-authorisation in 7 business days |

6.4 Summary of the longest pre-authorisation periods:
- (a) NEUR-CONS: none on any plan.
- (b) NEUR-MRI: 5 business days (MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE).
- (c) NEUR-NCS: 7 business days (CIVIC-BASE).

6.5 NEUR-NCS is the only procedure requiring pre-authorisation on all four plans, including MERIDIAN-GOLD.

6.6 NEUR-MRI is the only procedure with no pre-authorisation on MERIDIAN-GOLD alone among the plans where it is imaging; MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE all require it.

6.7 Pre-authorisation requests are submitted by the Neurology administration team once the procedure is ordered. Patients are not asked to submit requests themselves.

6.8 Procedures not listed in 6.2 are not part of this service line and are not scheduled under Neurology codes.

## 7. First-appointment commitment

7.1 The Neurology department commits to offering a first appointment **within 11 days** of an accepted referral.

7.2 The 11 days run from the acceptance date recorded under the Referral Management Policy. Referral intake and triage times before acceptance are governed by that policy and are not part of this commitment.

7.3 "Offered" means that a bookable slot is presented to the patient or referring office within 11 days. The commitment is met when the offer is made, not when the patient attends. A patient who declines an offered slot within the 11 days is treated as having been offered an appointment.

7.4 The commitment applies to the first Neurology Consultation (NEUR-CONS). It does not apply to NEUR-MRI or NEUR-NCS, whose scheduling depends on the pre-authorisation periods in section 6.3.

7.5 Where no slot can be offered within 11 days, the Head of Neurology is notified and the case is recorded as a missed commitment.
