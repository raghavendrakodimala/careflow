# Co-payment and Cost-Share Schedule

# MERIDIAN HEALTH PARTNERS
## Policy OPS-FIN-014: Member Cost-Share Schedule

**Owner:** Operations Policy
**Applies to:** All 14 Meridian sites, the billing office and the co-pay calculator
**Source of record:** Figures in this schedule match those held in the claims system. If the two ever differ, report it to Operations Policy and do not quote either figure to a patient until it is resolved.

---

### 1. Scope

1.1 This schedule sets the cost-share terms for the four plans administered by Meridian: MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE.

1.2 It covers the annual deductible, coinsurance rate, out-of-pocket maximum, specialist visit co-pay, telehealth co-pay, imaging co-pay and lab co-pay.

### 2. Plan Terms

2.1 Deductible, coinsurance and out-of-pocket maximum:

| Plan | Annual deductible | Coinsurance | Out-of-pocket maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| MERIDIAN-GOLD | $500 | 20% | $25,000 |
| MERIDIAN-SILVER | $1,500 | 30% | $15,000 |
| MERIDIAN-BRONZE | $3,000 | 40% | $8,000 |
| CIVIC-BASE | $2,000 | 35% | $10,000 |

2.2 Flat co-pays by service type:

| Plan | Specialist visit | Telehealth visit | Imaging | Lab work |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MERIDIAN-GOLD | $25 | $10 | $60 | $15 |
| MERIDIAN-SILVER | $45 | $18 | $110 | $25 |
| MERIDIAN-BRONZE | $45 | $18 | $160 | $35 |
| CIVIC-BASE | $45 | $18 | $130 | $30 |

2.3 Imaging and lab work each carry their own co-pay, separate from the specialist visit co-pay. Staff must not substitute the specialist co-pay for an imaging or lab service.

### 3. Telehealth

3.1 A telehealth visit carries a co-pay of exactly 40 percent of the plan's in-person specialist co-pay.

3.2 Applying clause 3.1 gives the figures in clause 2.2:

- MERIDIAN-GOLD: 40% × $25 = $10
- MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE: 40% × $45 = $18

### 4. Co-Pay Calculator Rules

4.1 **Flat-co-pay services.** A consultation, imaging or lab service costs the patient the flat co-pay in clause 2.2 only. No deductible or coinsurance is applied to these services.

4.2 **Procedures.** A procedure costs the patient:

- (a) the unmet part of the plan deductible, plus
- (b) the plan coinsurance rate applied to the remainder of the list price after the unmet deductible is subtracted,
- (c) capped so that the result is never more than the plan's out-of-pocket maximum.

4.3 **Unmet deductible.** The unmet deductible is the plan deductible minus the recorded deductible-met amount.

4.4 **Over-met deductible.** If the recorded deductible-met amount is above the plan's deductible, the deductible is fully met and the unmet deductible is $0. The calculator must not treat the excess as a credit and must not produce a negative unmet amount.

### 5. Worked Example: Partially Met Deductible

5.1 **Facts:** A MERIDIAN-BRONZE patient has met $1,800 of the $3,000 deductible. The patient has a Knee Arthroscopy (code ORTH-ARTH) with a list price of $6,200.

5.2 **Calculation:**

| Step | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Unmet deductible | $3,000 − $1,800 | $1,200 |
| 2. Remainder of list price | $6,200 − $1,200 | $5,000 |
| 3. Coinsurance on remainder | 40% × $5,000 | $2,000 |
| 4. Patient share before cap | $1,200 + $2,000 | $3,200 |
| 5. Out-of-pocket maximum check | $3,200 is below $8,000 | No cap applied |

5.3 **Result:** The patient owes **$3,200**.

### 6. Front-Desk Quoting

6.1 Quote flat co-pays for consultations, imaging and lab work exactly as shown in clause 2.2.

6.2 For procedures, quote the amount produced by the co-pay calculator. Do not calculate it by hand unless the calculator is unavailable, in which case use the method in clauses 4.2 to 4.4 and the worked example in clause 5.
