## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This handbook sets out what the Gastroenterology service line accepts, how long appointments take, what patients are told to do beforehand, which Meridian plans cover which services, and the department's own commitment on first-appointment timing.

1.2 It applies to all Meridian Health Partners sites at which Gastroenterology services are scheduled.

1.3 This handbook is an administrative document. It contains no diagnostic criteria or treatment guidance. Clinical decisions rest with the treating clinician.

1.4 Clinic-wide referral turnaround times are set by the Referral Management Policy. They are not restated here. Where this handbook and the Referral Management Policy appear to differ on referral processing times, the Referral Management Policy prevails.

## 2. Services provided

2.1 The service line provides three services, and only these three:

| Code | Service | Type | Duration | List price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GAST-CONS | Gastroenterology Consultation | Consultation | 30 minutes | $210 |
| GAST-USND | Abdominal Ultrasound | Imaging | 30 minutes | $520 |
| GAST-COLO | Colonoscopy | Procedure | 60 minutes | $2,600 |

2.2 Any procedure not listed in clause 2.1 is not provided by this service line and must not be booked under a GAST code.

2.3 Service durations in this specialty range from 30 minutes to 60 minutes.

## 3. Referrals: conditions accepted and not accepted

### 3.1 Referrals accepted

3.1.1 The service line accepts referrals for adults aged 16 years and over in the following categories:

(a) persistent or recurrent abdominal pain or discomfort;
(b) persistent change in bowel habit;
(c) persistent upper digestive symptoms, such as reflux, difficulty swallowing or recurrent nausea;
(d) abnormal liver blood-test results referred for specialist review;
(e) unexplained weight loss or iron-deficiency findings referred for gastrointestinal assessment;
(f) follow-up of previously diagnosed chronic digestive conditions where the patient has an existing Meridian record;
(g) bowel screening and surveillance referrals requesting colonoscopy;
(h) requests for abdominal ultrasound made by a referring clinician in connection with any of the categories above.

3.1.2 A referral is "accepted" when the Gastroenterology referral coordinator has confirmed in the scheduling system that it meets clause 3.1.1 and contains the information required in clause 3.3. The accepted date recorded at that point starts the clock for clause 6.1.

### 3.2 Referrals not accepted

3.2.1 The service line does not accept:

(a) patients under 16 years of age;
(b) emergency presentations, including suspected gastrointestinal bleeding, severe acute abdominal pain and signs of bowel obstruction. Referrers must direct these patients to emergency services and must not send them through the referral queue;
(c) pregnant patients referred for colonoscopy;
(d) patients referred for management of an existing liver transplant;
(e) patients referred for enteral or parenteral feeding management;
(f) patients requiring any procedure not listed in clause 2.1, including requests for upper endoscopy;
(g) self-referrals for GAST-USND or GAST-COLO. A GAST-CONS self-referral is accepted only if the patient holds an active Meridian plan;
(h) referrals marked "urgent" by the referrer without a stated reason.

3.2.2 A referral falling under clause 3.2.1 is returned to the referrer within the timeframe set by the Referral Management Policy, with the clause reference stated.

### 3.3 Minimum referral content

3.3.1 A referral is incomplete, and will not be accepted, unless it contains:

(a) the patient's full name, date of birth and Meridian or CIVIC member number;
(b) the service requested (GAST-CONS, GAST-USND or GAST-COLO);
(c) the referrer's name and contact number;
(d) a stated reason for referral of at least one sentence;
(e) a current medication list.

3.3.2 The referral coordinator contacts the referrer about incomplete referrals as set out in the Referral Management Policy.

## 4. Appointment durations

4.1 The standard new-patient consultation (GAST-CONS) is 30 minutes.

4.2 Abdominal Ultrasound (GAST-USND) is booked as 30 minutes.

4.3 Colonoscopy (GAST-COLO) is booked as 60 minutes.

4.4 Follow-up consultations are booked as GAST-CONS at 30 minutes. No separate follow-up code exists in this service line.

4.5 Durations in clauses 4.1 to 4.3 are the figures held in the scheduling system. Schedulers must not shorten or extend a booking without a change to the system record.

---

## 5. Plan coverage and pre-authorisation

5.1 Meridian operates four plans for the purposes of this service line: MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE.

5.2 All three services are covered under all four plans.

5.3 Pre-authorisation requirements and turnaround, in business days, are as follows:

| Service | MERIDIAN-GOLD | MERIDIAN-SILVER | MERIDIAN-BRONZE | CIVIC-BASE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GAST-CONS (30 min, $210) | Covered; none required | Covered; none required | Covered; none required | Covered; none required |
| GAST-USND (30 min, $520) | Covered; none required | Covered; pre-auth in 3 business days | Covered; pre-auth in 5 business days | Covered; pre-auth in 5 business days |
| GAST-COLO (60 min, $2,600) | Covered; pre-auth in 2 business days | Covered; pre-auth in 3 business days | Covered; pre-auth in 5 business days | Covered; pre-auth in 7 business days |

5.4 "Pre-auth in N business days" means pre-authorisation is required and the insurance team's turnaround for a decision is N business days. A booking requiring pre-authorisation is held as provisional until the authorisation is recorded.

5.5 The longest pre-authorisation turnaround in this service line is 7 business days (GAST-COLO under CIVIC-BASE). The shortest where pre-authorisation applies is 2 business days (GAST-COLO under MERIDIAN-GOLD).

5.6 Pre-authorisation requests for GAST-USND and GAST-COLO are submitted by the service line administrator on the day the referral is accepted.

5.7 List prices in clause 2.1 are the amounts held in the billing system. Patient cost-sharing is determined by plan terms and is not set by this handbook.

## 6. First-appointment commitment

6.1 The Gastroenterology service line commits to offering a first appointment within 9 days of the date a referral is accepted.

6.2 The commitment is to offer an appointment, not to complete it. If the patient declines the offered date, the offer remains compliant, and the declined offer must be recorded in the scheduling system.

6.3 Where a service requires pre-authorisation, the offer in clause 6.1 is made on the provisional booking basis described in clause 5.4.

6.4 The 9-day commitment is a department commitment. It is separate from, and does not alter, the referral processing times in the Referral Management Policy.

6.5 The operations manager reports compliance with clause 6.1 monthly. Compliance below 95% in any month requires a written recovery note to the Operations Policy lead within 10 business days of month end.

## 7. Preparation instructions given to patients

7.1 Preparation sheets are issued at the time of booking and again by message 3 days before the appointment. The sheet issued with a booking is the controlling version.

### 7.2 All appointments

(a) Arrive 15 minutes before the scheduled time.
(b) Bring photo identification, the plan membership card and the current medication list.
(c) Bring the referral letter if one was given to the patient.

### 7.3 Gastroenterology Consultation (GAST-CONS)

(a) No fasting is required.
(b) Patients are asked to bring any previous test results held outside Meridian.

### 7.4 Abdominal Ultrasound (GAST-USND)

(a) Patients are asked not to eat for 6 hours before the appointment.
(b) Water may be taken during the fasting period.
(c) Patients should wear loose clothing that allows access to the abdomen.

### 7.5 Colonoscopy (GAST-COLO)

(a) The bowel preparation kit and written schedule are issued by the department. Patients follow the schedule on the sheet exactly.
(b) A low-fibre diet begins 2 days before the procedure.
(c) Clear fluids only are taken for the whole day before the procedure.
(d) Patients must arrange for an adult escort to accompany them home. A patient without a named escort at check-in is rescheduled.
(e) Patients must not drive or operate machinery for 24 hours after the procedure.
(f) Patients must not stop or change any prescribed medication unless the department's clinical team has told them to do so.
(g) Patients unable to complete the preparation must call the department on the day before the procedure. The department may reschedule the booking.

7.6 Preparation instructions on the patient sheets are the only instructions the front-desk and scheduling teams may give. Staff must refer any clinical question about preparation to the service line nurse line.

## 8. Cancellation and rescheduling

8.1 Patients are asked to give at least 2 business days' notice to cancel or reschedule GAST-CONS and GAST-USND.

8.2 Patients are asked to give at least 5 business days' notice to cancel or reschedule GAST-COLO, because the preparation kit is issued and the 60-minute slot is held.

8.3 A rescheduled booking that is still within the pre-authorisation approval record does not require a new request.
