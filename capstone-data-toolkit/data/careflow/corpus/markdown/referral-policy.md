# Meridian Health Partners: Referral Management Policy

## Section 3: Referral Management

**3.1 Purpose and scope.** This section governs all referrals received by or issued from any of the 14 Meridian Health Partners sites, regardless of source. It applies to Referral Coordinators, Referral Hub staff, site clinicians and Medical Leads. Policy owner: Operations Policy Lead. Review cycle: every 12 months.

**3.2 Definitions.** "Business day" means Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00, excluding Meridian-observed public holidays. "Actioned" means the referral has been triaged, accepted or declined with reason, and an appointment offer or redirection has been recorded in the Meridian Referral Tracker (MRT). The SLA clock starts at the MRT receipt timestamp and stops when the referral is actioned.

**3.3 Emergent presentations.** Emergent presentations are never referred. Any staff member who identifies one must direct the patient to emergency care immediately, by calling the emergency number or initiating on-site emergency response, and must not create an MRT referral as a substitute. The event is logged in the incident system within 24 hours. Emergent presentations carry no referral SLA and cannot be downgraded into a referral band.

**3.4 Direct-referral specialties.** The following accept direct referrals without primary-care sign-off, from any Meridian clinician or from the patient where stated: Physiotherapy (patient self-referral permitted), Podiatry, Audiology, Optometry, Dietetics, Occupational Therapy, Dermatology, and Diagnostic Imaging (plain film and ultrasound only).

**3.5 Sign-off specialties.** The following require written sign-off by the patient's assigned primary-care physician before MRT will release the referral for triage: Cardiology, Gastroenterology, Neurology, Endocrinology, Rheumatology, Orthopaedics, Pain Management, Psychiatry, and Diagnostic Imaging (CT and MRI). Referrals lacking sign-off are returned to the sender within 1 business day with the reason "sign-off absent". The SLA clock starts only when sign-off is attached.

**3.6 Urgency bands.** Every referral is assigned exactly one band at intake by the referring clinician. The triaging clinician may re-band upward at any time and downward only with a recorded clinical reason.

**3.7 Routine SLA.** Routine referrals are actioned within 5 business days.

**3.8 Priority SLA.** Priority referrals are actioned within 2 business days.

**3.9 Urgent SLA.** Urgent referrals are actioned within 1 business day, that is, by the end of the next working day. An Urgent referral received at 16:40 on a Friday is due by 18:00 on the following Monday. MRT enforces these three bands and displays the remaining time to the assigned coordinator.

**3.10 Who may override an SLA.** Only the following roles may extend an SLA, and only within these limits:

- (a) Referral Hub Supervisor: Routine, up to 3 additional business days.
- (b) Site Medical Lead: Routine, up to 5 additional business days; Priority, up to 1 additional business day.
- (c) Regional Clinical Director: Routine and Priority, up to 5 additional business days; Urgent, up to 4 additional working hours.

No role may extend an Urgent SLA beyond 4 working hours. No role may shorten or cancel an SLA to suppress an alert. Coordinators and clinicians below Hub Supervisor level have no override authority.

**3.11 Override recording.** Each override must be entered in MRT with a reason code and the approver's user ID before the original SLA expires. An override entered after expiry is recorded as a breach with a late-justification flag, and the Chief Medical Officer's office reviews it in the monthly audit.

**3.12 Escalation ladder.** MRT triggers escalation automatically. Role titles and triggers are:

- (a) Level 1, Referral Coordinator: alerted when 80% of the SLA window has elapsed.
- (b) Level 2, Referral Hub Supervisor: alerted at breach.
- (c) Level 3, Site Medical Lead: alerted 1 business day after breach for Routine and Priority, or 4 working hours after breach for Urgent.
- (d) Level 4, Regional Clinical Director: alerted 3 business days after breach for Routine and Priority, or 1 business day after breach for Urgent.
- (e) Level 5, Chief Medical Officer: alerted 5 business days after breach for Routine and Priority, or 2 business days after breach for Urgent.

**3.13 Escalation duties.** The recipient at each level must acknowledge in MRT within 2 working hours and record a remedial action. An unacknowledged alert escalates to the next level without waiting for its stated trigger.

**3.14 Standard pathway.** Unless 3.15 to 3.17 apply, every referral follows the standard pathway: banding at intake (3.6), sign-off where required (3.5), triage, and actioning within the applicable SLA (3.7 to 3.9).

**3.15 Exception 1: Hospital discharge transfers.** A referral generated from an inpatient discharge summary issued by an external hospital may be accepted by a sign-off specialty without primary-care sign-off. The discharge summary must be attached and dated within the previous 14 days. The primary-care physician is notified within 2 business days of acceptance.

**3.16 Exception 2: Active oncology pathway.** A patient with a confirmed cancer diagnosis registered with the Meridian Oncology Coordination Office may be referred directly to any sign-off specialty by the Oncology Coordinator, with no primary-care sign-off. These referrals are banded no lower than Priority.

**3.17 Exception 3: Continuity re-referral.** A patient seen by a sign-off specialty at Meridian within the previous 12 months, for the same condition, may be re-referred once, directly, by the specialty's own clinic without primary-care sign-off. A second re-referral within that 12-month period requires sign-off under 3.5.

**3.18 No further exceptions.** Clauses 3.15 to 3.17 are the only documented exceptions to the standard pathway. Requests for any other deviation are declined. A request to the Regional Clinical Director for a policy amendment is the only route to add an exception, and amendments take effect only on publication of a revised Section 3.

**3.19 Audit and reporting.** The Operations Policy Lead publishes SLA compliance by site and band on the 5th business day of each month. The compliance target is 95% for each band. Any site below 90% in a band for two consecutive months must submit a written corrective plan to the Regional Clinical Director within 10 business days.

**3.20 Record retention.** MRT referral records, override entries and escalation acknowledgements are retained for 8 years from the date the referral was actioned.
