# ENT Service Line Handbook

# MERIDIAN HEALTH PARTNERS
## Service Line Handbook: Ear, Nose and Throat (ENT)

Document ID: MHP-OPS-SLH-ENT-01
Owner: Operations Policy
Department lead: Dr. Ilse Varnhagen
Applies to: all Meridian sites that schedule ENT services

---

### 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This handbook states what the ENT service line accepts and declines, how long its appointments run, what patients are told before attending, which plans cover which ENT services, and the department's commitment on first-appointment timing.

1.2 This handbook contains no diagnostic or treatment guidance. Clinical decisions rest with the treating clinician.

1.3 Referral intake, acknowledgement and triage turnaround times are set by the Referral Management Policy. This handbook does not restate them. Where this handbook and the Referral Management Policy appear to differ on referral processing times, the Referral Management Policy applies.

---

### 2. Services offered

2.1 The ENT service line offers three services only:

| Code | Service | Type | Duration | List price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ENT-CONS | ENT Consultation | Consultation | 20 minutes | $170 |
| ENT-AUDI | Audiometry | Lab | 30 minutes | $150 |
| ENT-SEPT | Septoplasty | Procedure | 90 minutes | $5,400 |

2.2 No other procedure, test or service is offered under the ENT service line. A request for any service not listed in 2.1 is declined at intake under clause 4.1(g).

---

### 3. Conditions accepted

3.1 The ENT service line accepts referrals for adult and adolescent patients aged 12 years and over where the referral describes one or more of the following:

(a) persistent nasal obstruction or nasal airflow complaints;
(b) hearing difficulty or reported change in hearing;
(c) recurrent or persistent ear complaints;
(d) recurrent or persistent throat or voice complaints;
(e) a request for hearing assessment, where the referrer has asked for audiometry;
(f) a referral from a Meridian clinician asking for assessment of suitability for septoplasty.

3.2 Audiometry (ENT-AUDI) may be booked with a consultation or on its own on a referral that names it.

3.3 Septoplasty (ENT-SEPT) is scheduled only after an ENT-CONS appointment in which the treating clinician has recommended it. It cannot be booked directly from a referral.

---

### 4. Conditions and requests not accepted

4.1 The following are declined and the referral is returned to the referrer with the reason recorded:

(a) patients under 12 years of age;
(b) referrals describing an emergency or time-critical presentation, which are not suitable for scheduled outpatient care;
(c) referrals for management of known or suspected head and neck malignancy;
(d) referrals for cosmetic or appearance-driven nasal procedures;
(e) referrals for implantable hearing devices;
(f) referrals for sleep-related breathing disorders where the request is for a procedure;
(g) any request for a service not listed in clause 2.1.

4.2 A declined referral is not a clinical judgement on the patient. It records that the request falls outside the ENT service line's scope.

---

### 5. Appointment durations

5.1 The standard new-patient consultation (ENT-CONS) is 20 minutes.

5.2 ENT services run between 20 and 90 minutes:

- ENT-CONS: 20 minutes
- ENT-AUDI: 30 minutes
- ENT-SEPT: 90 minutes

5.3 Scheduling holds these durations as fixed slot lengths. Front-desk staff must not shorten or extend a slot.

5.4 A patient booked for both ENT-CONS and ENT-AUDI on one visit is allocated 50 minutes (20 + 30).

---

### 6. First-appointment commitment

6.1 The ENT service line offers a first appointment within 6 days of an accepted referral.

6.2 The 6 days are calendar days, counted from the date the referral is recorded as accepted.

6.3 The commitment is that an appointment is offered within 6 days. It is not a commitment that the patient attends within 6 days, since the patient may choose a later date.

6.4 The commitment applies to the first ENT-CONS appointment, and to ENT-AUDI where a referral names audiometry alone. It does not apply to ENT-SEPT, which is scheduled after pre-authorisation under Section 8.

6.5 The time taken to accept a referral is governed by the Referral Management Policy and is outside this clause.

---

### 7. Patient preparation instructions

7.1 The following instructions are given to every patient at booking and repeated in the reminder message.

**All appointments**
(a) Arrive 15 minutes before the appointment time.
(b) Bring photo identification and the plan membership card (MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE or CIVIC-BASE).
(c) Bring a list of current medications.
(d) Bring any previous ENT or hearing reports held by the patient.

**ENT-AUDI (Audiometry)**
(e) Patients who use hearing aids must bring them.
(f) Allow 30 minutes for the appointment and 15 minutes for arrival, 45 minutes in total.

**ENT-SEPT (Septoplasty)**
(g) The patient must arrange an adult escort for the journey home. Check-in is not completed without the named escort's contact number.
(h) The pre-operative call from the surgical nursing team takes place no later than 2 business days before the procedure date.
(i) Eating, drinking and medication instructions for the day of surgery are issued only by the surgical and anaesthesia team in that call and in writing. Front-desk staff do not give or alter them.

7.2 Front-desk staff do not answer clinical questions about preparation beyond reading the instructions in 7.1. Such questions are passed to the ENT nursing line.

---

### 8. Plan coverage and pre-authorisation

8.1 Four plans are recognised: MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE.

8.2 All three ENT services are covered under all four plans.

8.3 Coverage and pre-authorisation matrix:

| Code | Service | List price | MERIDIAN-GOLD | MERIDIAN-SILVER | MERIDIAN-BRONZE | CIVIC-BASE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ENT-CONS | ENT Consultation | $170 | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation |
| ENT-AUDI | Audiometry | $150 | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation | Covered; no pre-authorisation |
| ENT-SEPT | Septoplasty | $5,400 | Covered; pre-authorisation in 2 business days | Covered; pre-authorisation in 3 business days | Covered; pre-authorisation in 5 business days | Covered; pre-authorisation in 7 business days |

8.4 "Pre-authorisation in N business days" means the plan's decision on a pre-authorisation request is returned within N business days of submission.

8.5 Pre-authorisation turnaround for ENT-SEPT by plan:

- MERIDIAN-GOLD: 2 business days
- MERIDIAN-SILVER: 3 business days
- MERIDIAN-BRONZE: 5 business days
- CIVIC-BASE: 7 business days

8.6 Pre-authorisation requests for ENT-SEPT are submitted on the same business day the treating clinician records the recommendation. A procedure date is not confirmed to the patient until the plan's decision is received.

8.7 ENT-CONS and ENT-AUDI are booked without any pre-authorisation step on any of the four plans.

8.8 List prices in Section 2 are the amounts held in the scheduling and insurance systems. Patient cost-sharing under each plan is set by the plan documents, not by this handbook.
