## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This handbook sets out the services offered by the Orthopaedics department, the referrals it accepts and declines, appointment durations, patient preparation instructions, plan coverage, and the department's first-appointment commitment.

1.2 It applies to all Meridian sites where Orthopaedics services are scheduled.

1.3 It contains administrative and scheduling information only. It does not give diagnostic or treatment guidance, which is the responsibility of the treating clinician.

1.4 Referral acceptance and referral turnaround times are governed by the **Referral Management Policy**. This handbook does not restate those times. Where this handbook and that policy appear to differ on referral turnaround, the Referral Management Policy applies.

---

## 2. Services Offered

2.1 The department offers exactly three services:

| Code | Service | Category | Duration | List price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ORTH-CONS | Orthopaedics Consultation | Consultation | 30 minutes | $200 |
| ORTH-MRI | MRI Knee | Imaging | 45 minutes | $1,400 |
| ORTH-ARTH | Knee Arthroscopy | Procedure | 75 minutes | $6,200 |

2.2 No other orthopaedic procedures or imaging types are scheduled under this service line.

---

## 3. Conditions Accepted

3.1 The department accepts referrals for adult patients (aged 18 or over) with non-urgent musculoskeletal conditions in the following scope:

- (a) **ORTH-CONS (consultation):** conditions of the knee, hip, shoulder and ankle.
- (b) **ORTH-MRI (imaging):** conditions of the knee only.
- (c) **ORTH-ARTH (procedure):** conditions of the knee only, following a completed ORTH-CONS.

3.2 A referral for ORTH-MRI or ORTH-ARTH must name the knee as the affected joint. Referrals that do not will be returned to the referrer for clarification.

3.3 Patients who have had orthopaedic surgery elsewhere are accepted for a consultation only if the surgery took place within the preceding 12 months and the operative report accompanies the referral.

---

## 4. Conditions Not Accepted

4.1 The department does not accept the following. Referrers must redirect these as stated in clause 4.2.

- (a) Patients under 18 years of age.
- (b) Acute fractures, open wounds over a joint, and injuries sustained within the previous 72 hours.
- (c) Suspected joint infection.
- (d) Spinal conditions.
- (e) Imaging or procedure requests for any joint other than the knee.
- (f) Post-operative review of surgery performed more than 12 months ago at another provider.
- (g) Medico-legal or independent medical examination requests.

4.2 Redirection routes:

- (a) Items 4.1(b) and 4.1(c) are directed to the nearest emergency department.
- (b) Items 4.1(a), 4.1(d), 4.1(f) and 4.1(g) are returned to the referrer with a written decline notice.
- (c) Item 4.1(e) is accepted for ORTH-CONS only; the consultation clinician decides onward routing.

---

## 5. Appointment Durations

5.1 The standard new-patient consultation is **30 minutes** (ORTH-CONS).

5.2 The department's services run from **30 to 75 minutes**: ORTH-CONS 30 minutes, ORTH-MRI 45 minutes, ORTH-ARTH 75 minutes.

5.3 Durations in clause 5.2 are the scheduled slot lengths held in the scheduling system. Check-in and preparation time is additional (see clause 6).

5.4 Follow-up consultations are booked as ORTH-CONS at 30 minutes. No shorter slot exists in this service line.

---

## 6. Patient Preparation Instructions

6.1 Instructions are issued to the patient by SMS and email at the time of booking, and repeated by SMS 48 hours before the appointment.

**All appointments**

6.2 Patients must arrive **15 minutes** before the scheduled start time and bring photo identification, their plan membership card, and any imaging reports or discs already held.

6.3 Patients should wear loose clothing that allows access to the affected joint.

**ORTH-MRI (MRI Knee)**

6.4 Patients must arrive **20 minutes** before the scheduled start to complete the safety screening form.

6.5 Patients must remove all metal items, including jewellery, belts, and hair clips, before entering the scanner room. Lockers are provided at no charge.

6.6 Patients with an implanted device or metal fragments must notify the Orthopaedics Scheduling Coordinator at least **3 business days** before the appointment.

**ORTH-ARTH (Knee Arthroscopy)**

6.7 Patients must stop eating solid food **8 hours** before the scheduled arrival time. Clear fluids are permitted until **2 hours** before arrival.

6.8 Patients must arrive **60 minutes** before the scheduled procedure start.

6.9 Patients must arrange for an adult to drive them home and remain with them for the first 24 hours.

6.10 A pre-procedure call from a nurse takes place **2 business days** before the procedure date. Patients who cannot be reached on two attempts will have the procedure rescheduled.

## 7. Plan Coverage and Pre-authorisation

7.1 Meridian offers four plans: MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE.

7.2 Coverage and pre-authorisation requirements for Orthopaedics services are as follows. "Pre-auth" shows the number of business days the plan takes to return a pre-authorisation decision.

| Code | MERIDIAN-GOLD | MERIDIAN-SILVER | MERIDIAN-BRONZE | CIVIC-BASE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ORTH-CONS | Covered, no pre-auth | Covered, no pre-auth | Covered, no pre-auth | Covered, no pre-auth |
| ORTH-MRI | Covered, no pre-auth | Covered, pre-auth in 3 business days | Covered, pre-auth in 5 business days | Covered, pre-auth in 5 business days |
| ORTH-ARTH | Covered, pre-auth in 2 business days | Covered, pre-auth in 3 business days | Covered, pre-auth in 5 business days | **Not covered** |

7.3 The pre-authorisation request is submitted by the department. The patient is not asked to submit it.

7.4 Where pre-authorisation applies, the appointment is not confirmed until approval is recorded in the insurance system.

7.5 ORTH-ARTH is not covered under CIVIC-BASE. The list price of **$6,200** applies if the patient chooses to proceed on a self-pay basis. The patient must sign the self-pay acknowledgement before a procedure date is offered.

7.6 List prices in clause 2.1 are the amounts before any plan adjustment and are held in the billing system.

---

## 8. First-Appointment Commitment

8.1 The Orthopaedics department commits to offering a first appointment **within 10 days** of an accepted referral.

8.2 The 10 days are counted as calendar days from the date the referral is recorded as accepted under the Referral Management Policy.

8.3 "Offered" means that at least one available slot has been communicated to the patient or referrer within the period. It does not require the patient to attend within 10 days.

8.4 If the patient declines the offered slot, the commitment is treated as met for that referral.

8.5 The commitment applies to the first ORTH-CONS appointment. It does not apply to ORTH-MRI or ORTH-ARTH, whose dates depend on the consultation outcome and on the pre-authorisation periods in clause 7.2.

8.6 Where no slot can be offered within 10 days, the Orthopaedics Scheduling Coordinator must notify the Head of Orthopaedics on the same business day.
