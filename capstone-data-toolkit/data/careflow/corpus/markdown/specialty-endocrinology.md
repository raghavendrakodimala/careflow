## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This handbook sets out what the Endocrinology department accepts and does not accept, how long its services take, what patients are told before attending, which Meridian plans cover which services, and the department's own commitment on first-appointment timing.

1.2 This handbook is an administrative document. It contains no diagnostic criteria or treatment guidance. Clinical decisions are made by the treating clinician.

1.3 Three services are within the scope of this handbook: ENDO-CONS, ENDO-PANL and ENDO-FNAB. No other procedure may be booked under the Endocrinology service line unless this handbook is formally amended.

---

## 2. Conditions accepted

2.1 The department accepts referrals for adult patients (aged 16 and over) where the stated reason for referral falls within one of the following groups:

- (a) Thyroid conditions, including goitre, thyroid nodules, and abnormal thyroid blood results.
- (b) Type 1 and type 2 diabetes mellitus where specialist review has been requested by the referring clinician.
- (c) Adrenal conditions.
- (d) Pituitary conditions.
- (e) Parathyroid and calcium-metabolism conditions.
- (f) Metabolic bone conditions, including osteoporosis referred for endocrine assessment.
- (g) Polycystic ovary syndrome where endocrine review is the stated reason for referral.

2.2 A referral must name at least one reason for referral from clause 2.1 to be considered for acceptance. Acceptance of the referral itself is governed by the Referral Management Policy.

---

## 3. Conditions explicitly not accepted

3.1 The department does not accept the following. Referrals of this kind are returned to the referrer with a written reason.

- (a) Patients under 16 years of age.
- (b) Gestational diabetes and any endocrine condition managed as part of an active obstetric pathway.
- (c) Active cancer treatment, including post-surgical oncology follow-up for thyroid cancer. These are directed to the oncology service.
- (d) Weight-management referrals where no endocrine condition is named.
- (e) Inpatient or post-hospital-discharge management requiring same-week review.
- (f) Requests for second-opinion review of imaging alone, with no patient attendance.

3.2 The Endocrinology department is not an emergency service. Patients who contact the department describing a medical emergency are directed to emergency services by the booking team. The department does not accept emergency referrals under any plan.

---

## 4. Appointment durations

4.1 The standard new-patient consultation is **30 minutes**.

4.2 Services in this specialty run between **15 and 30 minutes**, as follows:

| Code | Service | Type | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| ENDO-CONS | Endocrinology Consultation | Consultation | 30 minutes |
| ENDO-PANL | Thyroid Function Panel | Lab | 15 minutes |
| ENDO-FNAB | Thyroid Fine-Needle Aspiration | Procedure | 30 minutes |

4.3 Scheduling holds the full listed duration for each service. Durations in clause 4.2 are not shortened or extended by individual sites.

---

## 5. Preparation instructions given to patients

5.1 The booking team issues the following instructions at booking and again in the reminder message.

**All appointments**

5.2 Bring photo identification, the Meridian plan card (or CIVIC-BASE membership card), and a written list of all current medicines and supplements, including doses.

5.3 Arrive 10 minutes before the appointment time to complete check-in.

**ENDO-CONS (Endocrinology Consultation)**

5.4 Bring any previous thyroid, diabetes or bone-health results held by the patient, in paper or electronic form.

5.5 Bring a list of questions or concerns, as the 30-minute slot is fixed.

**ENDO-PANL (Thyroid Function Panel)**

5.6 Tell the booking team at the time of booking about any supplements taken, including biotin, so that the laboratory can be notified.

5.7 Patients are not asked to change any prescribed medicine for this test by the booking team. Questions about medicines are referred to the prescriber.

**ENDO-FNAB (Thyroid Fine-Needle Aspiration)**

5.8 Arrive 15 minutes before the appointment time, not 10, to allow for consent paperwork.

5.9 Bring the referral letter and any thyroid imaging report held by the patient.

5.10 Arrange for an adult to accompany the patient or to be contactable for collection after the appointment.

5.11 The appointment is confirmed only once pre-authorisation has been granted under section 6. Patients are told this at booking.

## 6. Plan coverage by service

6.1 Meridian operates four plans: MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE and CIVIC-BASE. All three services in this handbook are covered under all four plans.

6.2 List prices are the department's published prices before any plan adjustment.

| Code | Service | List price | GOLD | SILVER | BRONZE | CIVIC-BASE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ENDO-CONS | Endocrinology Consultation | $190 | Covered, no pre-auth | Covered, no pre-auth | Covered, no pre-auth | Covered, no pre-auth |
| ENDO-PANL | Thyroid Function Panel | $140 | Covered, no pre-auth | Covered, no pre-auth | Covered, no pre-auth | Covered, no pre-auth |
| ENDO-FNAB | Thyroid Fine-Needle Aspiration | $900 | Covered, pre-auth in 2 business days | Covered, pre-auth in 3 business days | Covered, pre-auth in 5 business days | Covered, pre-auth in 7 business days |

6.3 Pre-authorisation is required for ENDO-FNAB only. ENDO-CONS and ENDO-PANL do not require pre-authorisation under any plan.

6.4 The pre-authorisation periods in clause 6.2 are expressed in business days:

- (a) MERIDIAN-GOLD: 2 business days.
- (b) MERIDIAN-SILVER: 3 business days.
- (c) MERIDIAN-BRONZE: 5 business days.
- (d) CIVIC-BASE: 7 business days.

6.5 The department requests pre-authorisation for ENDO-FNAB as soon as the referral is accepted and the procedure is requested by the clinician. The patient is not required to request it.

## 7. First-appointment commitment

7.1 The department commits to offering a first appointment within **12 days** of an accepted referral, counted from the date the referral is accepted.

7.2 This is the Endocrinology department's own commitment on first-appointment offer. It is separate from, and does not replace, the clinic-wide referral turnaround times. Those times are set by the Referral Management Policy and are not restated in this handbook. Where this handbook and the Referral Management Policy appear to differ on referral turnaround, the Referral Management Policy governs.

7.3 An appointment is "offered" when the patient has been given at least one specific date and time. The commitment is met by the offer, not by the patient's acceptance of it.

7.4 For ENDO-FNAB, the 12-day commitment applies to the offer of the first appointment. It does not shorten the pre-authorisation periods in clause 6.4.
